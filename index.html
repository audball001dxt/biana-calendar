Biana Calendar Assistant

A personal-use calendar assistant built with Hermes Agent. It helps its owner and authorized users review schedules, receive daily and weekly calendar summaries, and manage calendar events when explicitly requested and confirmed.

Google Calendar access

The app requests permission to read calendars and events available to the authorizing account, and to create or edit events on calendars where that account has appropriate permissions. Access is restricted by Google Calendar permissions.

Calendar information may be processed by the assistant’s configured AI service and delivered through Telegram to destinations authorized by the owner. This public website does not display calendars and does not offer a public sign-in service.

Control and contact

The owner can revoke Google access at any time in Google Account connections. Authorized users can contact the app owner through their existing assistant conversation or the support email displayed on the Google consent screen.

Please read the privacy policy before authorizing access.
