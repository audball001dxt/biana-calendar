Privacy policy

This policy describes Biana Calendar Assistant, a personal-use application operated by its owner using Hermes Agent. It is not a Google product and is not a public calendar service.

Information accessed

With authorization, the app can access Google Calendar metadata, calendar permissions and event information such as titles, times, locations, descriptions, attendees and event status. Event changes are limited by the permissions granted and the relevant calendar’s access controls. OAuth credentials are used to maintain authorized access.

Purpose and use

Calendar data is used to answer authorized scheduling questions, prepare daily or weekly briefs, identify possible conflicts, and perform event changes explicitly requested and confirmed by the user. Calendar API access does not itself authorize unrelated access to Gmail, Drive or Microsoft Outlook.

Processing and delivery

Calendar API requests are sent to Google. Calendar information included in an assistant request or summary may be processed by the AI provider configured by the owner. Briefs and responses may be delivered through Telegram, including owner-authorized groups. Only destinations authorized for the relevant information should receive it. Google, the configured AI provider and Telegram process information under their respective terms and privacy policies.

Storage and retention

OAuth credentials are stored in access-restricted files on the owner’s assistant system. Calendar information may also be retained in local conversation history, generated briefing files, operational logs or backups and in delivered Telegram messages. There is no automatic deletion period promised by this policy; retention depends on the owner’s assistant configuration and cleanup practices. Revoking access stops future authorized Google API access but does not automatically erase information already stored or delivered.

Restrictions

The application does not sell Google Calendar data or use it for advertising. Information is used for the authorized assistant functions described here, not to train a generalized AI model by the application. Use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Provider settings and terms must be consistent with these restrictions; this policy does not substitute for configuring them appropriately.

Your choices

You may revoke access through Google Account connections. To request deletion of assistant-held information or ask a privacy question, contact the owner through your existing assistant conversation or the support email shown on the consent screen. Deletion from the local assistant does not automatically delete copies held in Telegram, provider systems or backups; those require separate applicable deletion controls.

This website

These pages contain no calendar data, OAuth credentials, advertising scripts or analytics added by the application. If hosted using GitHub Pages, GitHub may collect technical access information under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Policy changes

The owner will update this page if the application’s data practices change materially.
